Quick answers
  • What is it? A free open-source extension that names framing techniques (loaded language, omission, source selection, and more).
  • What's new in 2.1.1? Neutrality 0 stays 0. Local calibration on heuristics. Gold suite 20/20. Grades-card PNG/JPEG stays on-device.
  • Need a key? No for heuristics. Full AI is BYOK xAI Grok.
  • Paywalls? No bypass. DOM text or lawful paste only.

Features

  • Multi-pass analysis (structure → detect → verify → optional context)
  • Signal Radar 2.0 and Technique Academy 2.0
  • Letter grades A+ - F from calibrated neutrality - techniques over tribes
  • Outlet and journalist scoreboards from your local scans
  • Research brief Markdown / print export
  • Grades-card PNG or JPEG, rendered on-device

Shortcuts

  • Ctrl+Shift+B shades on/off
  • Ctrl+Shift+Y side panel
  • Alt+[ / Alt+] navigate signals
  • Alt+R local reader extract
